ABOUT

WHERE IDENTITY
BREAKS INTO ART.

FRAGMENTED SOULS explores the pieces we carry: memories, contradictions, emotions and the versions of ourselves that change over time.

Each work is built as a contemporary visual fragment — geometric, tactile and deliberately imperfect.

FRAGMENTED SOULS is a digital art project transforming complex emotions into timeless visual fragments.

The collection combines contemporary cubist principles, layered geometry and a tactile museum-inspired surface.
